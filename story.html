ABOUT SUDH VAISHNO TANDOOR

Fresh food. Honest prices. A taste of home.

Since 1996, Sudh Vaishno Tandoor has been serving pure vegetarian food in Chandigarh with a simple promise — fresh food, honest prices and a taste that feels like home.

From hot rotis and parathas to dal, sabji and rice, we prepare everyday favourites for students, families, hospital visitors and local customers.
